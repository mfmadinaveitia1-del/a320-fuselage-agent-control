Goal

why this twin exists

Make A320 final assembly more efficient: reach takt at 10.5 aircraft per month, deliver every aircraft within its committed lead time, and remove rework and travelled work at the source. The twin shows where time is lost and tests each fix before it reaches the shop floor.
