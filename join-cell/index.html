Dashboard objective why this screen exists

Provide a unified, real-time view of A320 fuselage assembly performance, ensuring production remains on track in terms of quality, cost, and delivery. The dashboard enables early identification of critical path deviations, elimination of non-value-added activities, and optimization of manufacturing processes through Lean principles, including Kanban, Just-in-Time (JIT), and Just-in-Sequence (JIS).

By integrating AI Agents, Edge AI, Digital Twins, Industrial Metaverse, and Cloud technologies, the solution enables proactive detection and prediction of supply chain, quality, and production disruptions. This supports faster, data-driven decision-making, minimizes downtime, reduces scrap and rework, and improves right-first-time performance, contributing to the production target of 100 aircraft per month.

The dashboard also provides end-to-end visibility into multi-agent orchestration, illustrating how specialized AI agents collaborate, exchange information, coordinate decisions, and execute complex, multi-step workflows as a unified, goal-driven system. This transparency enables stakeholders to monitor agent activities, understand automated decisions, track issue resolution, and maintain human oversight of critical manufacturing operations.

  • Deliver on taktEach shipset closes inside its 70 s cycle plan (time-scaled for the simulation).
  • Zero escapesEdge vision inspects every fastener before the section moves on.
  • No surprisesAnomalies are flagged, re-planned and verified in the twin within seconds.

KPIs measure how well the cell performed. KRIs (key risk indicators) are early warnings that a KPI is about to be missed. The agents watch the KRIs continuously and act on them; people see the same values here.

      How the layers map

      Multi-agent cell

      Each robot is an agent that owns a queue of fastener holes. When a queue runs dry it negotiates work from a neighbour on the same rail. The Cell Supervisor arbitrates pauses, rework and hand-overs.

      Digital twin

      Cyan ghosts mirror every arm at 50 Hz. No re-plan reaches the shop floor until the twin runs a virtual-commissioning pass for paths and clashes, the role Process Simulate plays in a Siemens Tecnomatix line.

      Edge AI

      Hole-quality vision and spindle-vibration models run on industrial edge devices beside the cell. Defects and bearing wear are caught in milliseconds with no cloud round trip, as Siemens Industrial Edge apps do.

      Industrial metaverse

      All layers composed in one live 3D space where engineers, agents and the physical cell share one model. Siemens positions Xcelerator with NVIDIA Omniverse for this; the full view above is a sketch of it.
